    Practice mode

    The whole job, every day

    No R, no macros, no Power Query, nothing to install. Open the link, type numbers, and it is saved. The weather fills itself in.

    First time only — add your plants

    1. Go to Plants and add each one: the label you wrote on the pot, its treatment group, when it was sown, how deep, how many seeds.
    2. Adding a full set at once is quicker — Add a full set… creates numbered plants split evenly across the treatment groups.
    3. You can rename, re-assign or retire a plant later without losing a single reading.

    Each visit to the plants

    1. Open the link. Check that Logging as at the top right says your name.
    2. The date is already today. Set Time measured.
    3. The green band under the date says what is due today — height and leaves every day, SPAD on Fridays, the ceptometer every other Friday, and both again three days after a dose.
    4. Work down the plant cards and type the numbers. Boxes that are due today are outlined in green; anything not due is still there under also record… if you take it anyway.
    5. There is no save button. Each box saves about a second after you stop typing and the card turns green with a ✓.

    Under every box is a small arrow — green up, red down — comparing what you just typed with that plant's last reading of the same thing. A red arrow is not an error, it is the plant telling you something.

    Dosing days

    1. Click Record a dose in the orange strip.
    2. Pick the date, choose which plants (a whole treatment group in one click), and confirm the strength and volume — 100 mL at 2% or 5% is pre-filled.
    3. The app then reminds you that SPAD and the ceptometer are due three days later, and every chart marks the dose day.

    Looking at one plant

    Click any plant's name. You get its full history, its growth curves with the dose days marked, and every reading with the change from the time before.

    Getting it into Excel

    1. Go to Data & export.
    2. Optional: narrow the dates, one plant, or one treatment group.
    3. Download Excel gives you one workbook: growth readings, a per-day summary, treatment means, the dosing log, the weather, your plant list, and a data dictionary explaining every column.

    Every growth row already carries that day's weather and heat units beside it, so there is no VLOOKUP to do. Weather has its own export if you only want the conditions.

    The status email

    Status update writes the summary for you. Pick a window — last 7 days, last 30 days, or the whole trial — and it rewrites instantly: where every group stands now, how far it moved, what was dosed, the weather, and anything heading the wrong way. Press Copy to clipboard and paste it into an email.

    Your working file

    Upload your analysis workbook under Files whenever you update it. Anyone you send this link to can then see the live data and download your latest analysis without you emailing anything.

    Fixing a number you already saved

    Type over it, or use clear to remove a reading entirely. If the value had already been committed — you saved it on an earlier visit, or someone else recorded it — a short box appears asking for your initials, the date, and a one-line reason. Nothing is saved until you fill it in, and Cancel puts the original value back.

    Correcting something you typed in the last few minutes does not ask — that is just finishing your entry. It only asks once a reading has settled, and after a reading has been formally corrected once it will always ask again.

    This is not bureaucracy for its own sake: it means every number in your dataset either came straight off the instrument or has a documented reason for being different. Corrections show up on the plant's own page, on the Corrections sheet of the Excel export, and in the permanent record. The original value is never lost.

    Things worth knowing

    • It works without signal. Anything typed in the greenhouse is held on the phone and syncs when you have bars again. The pill at the top right says when something is waiting.
    • Blank is not zero. Leaving a box empty means you did not measure it. Zero means you measured zero.
    • Leaves are a total count — how many are on the plant right now, not how many appeared. A drop from 6 to 4 is real data.
    • Nothing is deleted by accident. The only removal is the small "clear" link on one card, and it asks first. Retiring a plant keeps all its history.
    • Practice first. Setup › Load sample data gives you a full fake trial to click around in, and clearing it leaves your real data untouched.
    • It backs itself up. Twice a day everything is written into permanent files on GitHub — every reading, your uploaded analysis files, and a log of who changed what and when. Setup › Permanent record shows when that last happened. You cannot lose this data by dropping your phone.
